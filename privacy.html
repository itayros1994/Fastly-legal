Privacy Policy (Fastly)

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Fastly ("we", "the app") is an intermittent fasting timer. We designed it to keep your data on your device.

1. Data we process

  • Fasting sessions, plans, and statistics you create in the app are stored locally on your iPhone (e.g. SwiftData / on-device storage).
  • Purchase/subscription status is handled by Apple StoreKit. We do not operate our own account system and we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • If you allow notifications, iOS schedules local notifications on your device. We do not use a separate push server for these alerts.

2. Data we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or email.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking in the current version.

3. Health information

Fastly is a tracking tool only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Any fasting-related entries stay on your device unless you choose to share them (e.g. screenshots).

4. Children's privacy

Fastly is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

5. Your choices

  • Delete app data by deleting the app (or clearing app data via iOS).
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions (or in-app Manage Subscription).
  • Control notification permission in iOS Settings.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do.
